# Grammar drill

Teach one concept, set one English sentence, grade the learner's Portuguese.

1. **Pick the concept.** If the argument names a concept or lesson, use it. Otherwise read the screenshots and the log, and pick one concept the syllabus covers that the log shows as least practised or last missed. Done when you can name one concept, drawn from a screenshot, in a few words.
2. **Teach it.** The rule, two or three short Portuguese examples with English glosses, and the common trap.
3. **Set the sentence.** One English sentence that exercises the concept. Ask for it in Portuguese, then stop and wait.
4. **Grade the answer.**
   - **Correct answer**: the model sentence, plus any equally valid alternatives.
   - **Verdict**: correct, or the list of mistakes. For each: what they wrote, the fix, and the rule behind it, tied back to the concept where it applies. Count accents, gender/number agreement, verb form, word order and spelling; a valid alternative phrasing counts as correct.
5. **Log it** and offer the next round.
